> subject, not a regulated system — to NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 and 21 CFR Part 11 identifiers, and says what the evidence does

> arguable mappings — is **AI-drafted** (Claude) under Amendment A2 and is not the author's writing. What is not drafted: each
> quoted control statement is verbatim from the primary source (`rubric/framework-sources.json`), every number is computed from
> the evidence, and every cited Test ID resolves to a record of the official run. Generated by `npm run attest wp6`; never edited.

## Support levels

- **evidenced (tested paths only)** — the tested behaviour is what the statement describes and every cited record held (no deficient, no gap). Tested paths only — never a statement that the control is met.
- **partial** — the evidence bears on part of what the statement asks, or cited records include deficient or gap results, or the relationship is an analogy. Every Part 11 provision and every activity of Attest itself is capped here.
- **not mapped** — no Attest evidence supports a mapping; nothing is cited as evidence.
